What we built

An agent that does the legwork

  2. Search & readTheir county first, then Florida: … open contracts and … grants. It reads the promising ones and rules out set-asides they can't bid on.

Claude decides which tools to call and in what order. The page shows every step it takes, and nothing leaves without a person's click.

How it's built

Claude does the reasoning. Public data does the rest.

  1. Live public dataEvery open federal notice from the SAM.gov daily extract, Miami-Dade County's own bid feeds, Florida state solicitations, Grants.gov, and USAspending award history.
  2. An agent with 7 toolsClaude decides which to call: search contracts, read one, find competitors, find expiring work, search grants, save to the shortlist, propose an email.
  3. Action, with approvalThe buyer email lands in the owner's Gmail as a draft through Claude's Gmail connector, only after they click.
  4. Text itOwners can text their personal AI agent. It writes the question to a shared Google Doc, and govbid answers there within five minutes.

Plain JavaScript, no framework. 33 automated tests. Map outlines drawn from US Census boundaries.

Business model

Search is free. The agent working for you is not.

Free

Search everything

Public data stays public. Anyone can search, map and track contracts. This is how people find us.

$99 / month

The agent works for you

Matched contracts in your trade every week, the buyer contacted in your name, and a warning before work you could take over expires.

A flat subscription, never a cut of the contract: federal rules (FAR 52.203-5) restrict fees that depend on winning. Competitors charge $1,350 to $29,000 a year and sell to companies that already bid. General personal agents like Instinct do errands for anyone; this one does one job, government contracting, for the businesses those tools ignore.

How we roll it out

Start by hand. Then let it run.

  1. Month 1We run the agent ourselves for 10 Miami contractors, review every email, and send them one real contract each. Free.
  2. Month 2–3The ones who bid become the first $99 subscribers. We track bids submitted and contracts won.
  3. ThenSelf-serve: a contractor signs up with their trade and zip code, and the agent runs on its own with their approval.

Impact

We count one thing: dollars won by local businesses.

Every contract a Miami small business wins is public money that stays in Miami: payroll, jobs, and a track record that makes the next bid easier.

It points every business to free local help: the Florida APEX Accelerator at FIU, Miami-Dade SBE certification, and the county supplier portal. The agent answers in the owner's own language: English, Spanish or Haitian Creole. Next: City of Miami, Miami-Dade schools and Water & Sewer feeds, and texting the agent.
